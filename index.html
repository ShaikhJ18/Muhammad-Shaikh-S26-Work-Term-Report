Muhammad Shaikh's Summer 2026 Work Term Report


Introduction

Hi there! My name is Muhammad Shaikh. During the Summer 2026 semester, I worked as a Student Project Coordinator at AtkinsRéalis within the Candu Energy division, supporting the Project Delivery team through technical, organizational, and process-improvement projects. My work involved developing Power BI dashboards, internal database tools using Access, VBA, and SQL, and Excel-based reporting solutions. I also collaborated with teams including HR and the machine shop to improve reporting and workflows, while gaining experience presenting solutions to senior engineering teams. Beyond my technical work, I helped onboard new students, taught Power BI during a Lunch and Learn, and contributed to STEM outreach and co-op initiatives. This work strengthened my skills in data analysis, communication, collaboration, leadership, and project coordination.

Employer

AtkinsRéalis is a global engineering and project management company that delivers solutions across industries including nuclear energy, infrastructure, transportation, and engineering services. I worked within the Candu Energy division, which is responsible for maintaining, building, and refurbishing CANDU nuclear reactors that provide a large portion of Ontario’s electricity. The division supports major nuclear projects across Canada and internationally, helping deliver reliable and clean energy through advanced reactor technology and engineering solutions.

Job Description

In my role as a Student Project Coordinator at Candu Energy, I worked within the project delivery team supporting reporting, internal operations, and coordination across multiple ongoing nuclear projects. I used Power BI, Microsoft Excel, VBA, Microsoft Access, SQL, and SharePoint to develop dashboards, reporting tools, and database solutions that helped teams track information, improve efficiency, and support decision-making. I worked closely with various teams, including the machine shop, to coordinate efficiency initiatives through Power BI reporting, organize meetings, and support process improvements. I also collaborated with HR to understand their workflows and develop tools that improved their processes and information management. In addition to developing internal tools, I completed ad hoc reporting requests, maintained project information, and created SharePoint resources to improve communication across the organization. During the summer semester, I helped onboard new students and taught them Power BI through the final Lunch and Learn presentation. I also organized a group co-op photo and article that was shared on the company’s internal site and LinkedIn channels. Through these experiences, I strengthened my technical, communication, organizational, and leadership skills while gaining a broader understanding of how different teams collaborate within a large engineering organization.

Conclusions

My experience at Candu Energy allowed me to grow beyond my technical responsibilities and become a more confident communicator, collaborator, and problem solver. Building reporting and database solutions taught me how technology can improve real organizational processes, while working with HR, the machine shop, and senior engineering teams showed me the importance of understanding the people and workflows behind the data. Helping onboard students and teaching Power BI also gave me an opportunity to share what I had learned and develop my leadership skills. Overall, this work term gave me a stronger understanding of how computing can support large engineering organizations and provided experiences that I will carry into future co-op and professional opportunities.
